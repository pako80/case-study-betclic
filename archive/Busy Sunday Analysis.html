Live football across the day

A match counts as live for 107 minutes after kick-off (two halves, stoppage and the break). Yellow marks the peak.

Tennis

  • No Grand Slam on the coming Sundays. The last one, the US Open, ended 13 Sep. The next are the Australian Open in January and Roland-Garros from 24 May.
  • Nitto ATP Finals, Turin, 15–22 Nov. Sunday 15 Nov is its opening day, so it leads tennis in the lobby. Sessions around 14:00 and 21:00 CET.
  • Slam Sundays do matter. Roland-Garros, the US Open and the Australian Open play on their middle Sunday, with a full round of singles on the big courts. Wimbledon's middle Sunday is a rest day. On those dates the Slam should lead Hot and Tennis; the Roland-Garros tweak already does this.
  • Challenger and WTA 125 events fill the rest: 5 live at the peak, 2 in the evening.

Time zones

  • One live list for everyone. Only the peak moves, depending on where your players are.
  • Brazil (BRT): CET minus 4 h in winter, minus 5 h in summer. The European peak is late morning there. Brasileirão kicks off at 20:00 CET (16:00 BRT), so Brazil forms the evening wave.
  • UK: CET minus 1 h. US East: CET minus 6 h, so NBA Sunday games land in the evening wave.
  • Japan: CET plus 8 h. J1 League is live around 06:00 CET, before the peak, so it is not in the lobby.
